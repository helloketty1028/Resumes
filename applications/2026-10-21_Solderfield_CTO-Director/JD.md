We invite qualified technology professionals to join us as business partners to transform banking solutions and project expertise into repeatable, configurable software products with clear intellectual property ownership, reliable support, and measurable customer value. This initiative also encompasses solution platform development and architecture modernisation.

Proven experience and expertise in banking solutions are essential. Through productisation, we aim to build a scalable and sustainable software solutions business.

Qualified professionals with a serious interest in this partnership opportunity are invited to submit their professional profiles, relevant credentials, and details of their banking technology experience.

## Responsibilities

The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) Business Partner shall be responsible for leading the technical and commercial development of the software solutions business, from opportunity identification and solution design through negotiation, delivery and project closure.

1. **Technology Strategy and Product Development** — Define the technology roadmap, solution platforms and architecture modernisation strategy. Convert banking domain knowledge and project expertise into reusable, configurable software products with clear intellectual property ownership.
2. **New Solution Design and Proposals** — Lead requirements analysis, solution architecture, feasibility assessments and proof-of-concept activities. Prepare proposals covering scope, deliverables, implementation approach, resources, timelines, costs and customer value.
3. **Business Opportunity Acquisition** — Identify and pursue new opportunities, develop client and strategic partner relationships, and lead technical presentations, demonstrations and tender responses to secure business.
4. **Commercial and Contract Negotiation** — Lead or support negotiations on pricing, scope, payment milestones, acceptance criteria, intellectual property rights, support obligations and contractual responsibilities, within agreed approval authority.
5. **Solution and Project Delivery** — Oversee implementation, resource allocation, technical quality, security, testing and deployment. Manage project risks, dependencies, scope changes and client expectations to achieve agreed delivery commitments and profitability.
6. **Project Closure and Commercial Completion** — Secure client acceptance and completion acknowledgements, complete documentation and operational handover, and coordinate final invoicing and payment follow-up. Review project outcomes, lessons learned and reusable product components.
7. **Ongoing Support and Business Growth** — Establish reliable maintenance and support arrangements, drive product improvements, and develop opportunities for renewals, enhancements and wider adoption of the solutions.

## Qualifications

1. **Banking Domain Expertise** — Proven experience in banking technology and solutions is mandatory, with strong knowledge of banking operations, transaction processing, integration requirements and delivery standards.
2. **Technology Leadership** — Demonstrated experience as a CTO, Solution Architect, Technical Director or equivalent senior technology leader, with responsibility for architecture, engineering teams and solution delivery.
3. **Solution Architecture and Modernisation** — Strong capability in designing secure, scalable and resilient solutions, including middleware, APIs, microservices, message transformation, workflow automation and legacy system modernisation.
4. **Software Productisation** — Proven ability to convert project-specific implementations into reusable, configurable software products, with sound practices for intellectual property ownership, documentation, version management and ongoing support.
5. **Proposal and Business Development** — Ability to identify business opportunities, engage clients, assess requirements and develop compelling solution proposals, demonstrations and proofs of concept.
6. **Commercial and Negotiation Skills** — Experience in solution costing, pricing, contract negotiation and defining scope, payment milestones, acceptance criteria and support commitments. Ability to balance customer requirements with business profitability.
7. **End-to-End Delivery Experience** — A successful track record of managing projects from requirements and design through development, testing, deployment, client acceptance, operational handover and project closure.
8. **Governance and Quality Management** — Strong understanding of secure development, access controls, audit trails, change management, testing, release governance and operational resilience within banking environments.
9. **Leadership and Partnership Commitment** — An entrepreneurial mindset, professional integrity and willingness to take ownership of business outcomes. Ability to build teams, collaborate with fellow business partners and contribute actively to long-term business growth.
10. **Education and Professional Credentials** — A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related discipline is preferred. Equivalent substantial industry experience will also be considered; relevant professional certifications are advantageous.
