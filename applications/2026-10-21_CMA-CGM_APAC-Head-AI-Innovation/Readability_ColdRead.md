## Suggestions (wording, order and cuts only)

1. **Rewrite the eMemo sentence.**
   Now: "Personally built an AI agent that generates validation prompts to check schema alignment and data-relationship consistency between eMemo and quotation documents, removing a recurring manual reconciliation task."
   Propose: "Personally built an AI agent that checks eMemo and quotation documents for consistency with each other, removing a recurring manual reconciliation task."

2. **Plain-English the Applied AI competency line.**
   Now: "LLM agents on headless Claude Code, agentic workflow orchestration, prompt A/B evaluation, AgentOps/LLMOps practice, taking AI tools through formal approval into production"
   Propose: "AI agents built on Claude Code; comparing prompt versions to improve quality (AgentOps/LLMOps); taking AI tools through formal bank approval into production". Drop "agentic workflow orchestration", which repeats the previous clause.

3. **Fix and shorten the schedule-conflict bullet.**
   Now: "When the two platform lines' schedules conflict, agree MVP scope with the requirement, project and technical leads, confirm dependencies and set a sequence all parties accept."
   Propose: "Resolve schedule conflicts between the two platform lines by agreeing MVP scope and delivery order with the requirement, project and technical leads."

4. **Cut or merge the jargon bullets.**
   - Merge the KPI bullet: "Established delivery KPIs (deployment frequency, recovery time, incident trend, change-failure rate), CI/CD quality gates and test sign-off for every release." Drop "99% SonarQube coverage gate, automated regression testing".
   - Merge the P1–P3 bullet into: "Coordinate production incidents end to end, from escalation to root-cause analysis and post-incident reports." Drop "with no separate SRE team, the platform teams own production support and monitoring".
   - Shorten the ISO 20022 bullet: "Owned delivery of the ISO 20022 (SWIFT MT-to-MX) payment-message migration with a 15-engineer team: live in May 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November 2025 deadline, with no service interruption."
   - Cutting these frees space so the page 3 break falls between jobs.

5. **Order.** Move "Hold decision or joint-decision authority..." and "Report status, risks and decisions to senior management and steering committees..." to the top of Delivery & Governance. The JD stresses senior-stakeholder influence. Move "Coach engineering teams on AI-assisted engineering practices and tooling" up beside the first Applied AI bullet, so enablement is seen early.

6. **Summary.** "Used to explaining technical choices in business terms..." becomes "Experienced in explaining technical choices in business terms to senior management and steering committees, and in taking part in vendor-selection and technology-investment decisions."

7. **Ownership wording.** Make the UOB bullet "Took the AI code-review service into production..." match the summary's "designed and built". For example, "Designed, built and took the AI code-review service into production: ..." so the two places agree.
