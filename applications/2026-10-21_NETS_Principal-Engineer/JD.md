The NETS Group is a leading payments services group, enabling digital payments for merchants, consumers and banks across the entire payments value chain.

The Group operates Singapore's national debit scheme enabling customers of DBS Bank/POSB, HSBC, Maybank, OCBC Bank, Standard Chartered Bank and UOB to make payments using their ATM cards or mobile devices at more than 130,000 acceptance points in the country as well as online payments.

## Position Summary

We are seeking a Principal Engineer to serve as the application development and delivery lead, as well as the senior technical authority, for the UPOS & DA application portfolio. The role is responsible for defining the target architecture, engineering strategy, technology standards, modernization roadmap, and major solution decisions for mission-critical digital payment platforms and Java-based backend applications.

The successful candidate will guide a dynamic technical team in designing and delivering secure, scalable, high-performance, and highly available payment solutions. The role is accountable for driving the speed, quality, reliability, and operational resilience of application delivery while ensuring that solutions align with business objectives, cybersecurity standards, payment regulations, and industry best practices.

As an effective communicator, the candidate must be able to translate complex business requirements into clear technical strategies and solution architectures, communicate design decisions effectively to engineering teams and senior stakeholders, and provide direction across multiple concurrent development tracks. The role will collaborate closely with Product Development, Business Development, Technology, Architecture, Cybersecurity, Infrastructure, QA, DevOps, Operations, Finance, vendors, banks, and payment schemes to ensure successful delivery from concept through production.

The ideal candidate will possess deep application engineering and payment-domain expertise, strong executive-level communication and influencing skills, and substantial hands-on software development experience. The candidate must be able to establish engineering guardrails, manage technical risks, guide complex transformation programmes, mentor senior engineers, and remain hands-on when resolving high-impact architectural, delivery, and production challenges.
