## (a) HR recruiter, 30 seconds

**1. Who is he.** Senior technology leader with 18+ years. He is a Vice President (VP2) / Technical Delivery Manager at UOB, a MAS-regulated bank, since Oct 2022. He leads about 25 engineers on cross-border payments and retail lending. He has earlier head-of-technology, project-manager and architect roles at start-ups. The top of page 1 reads as "senior engineering leader who still codes in Java, from a bank". The headline says "Head of Technology Development", but the actual current title is VP2 / Technical Delivery Manager.

**2. Three strongest matches to the JD.**
1. **Regulated financial services.** The summary opens on a "MAS-regulated Tier-1 bank". MAS TRM, DPIA, regulatory incident reporting and secure SDLC all recur. This matches "financial services and/or strict regulatory frameworks" and "technology in compliance with regulations".
2. **Leading multi-discipline teams while running BAU and change in parallel, to deadlines.** The summary line "leads multi-discipline teams and delivers BAU and change projects to deadline" mirrors the JD. It is backed by the ISO 20022 delivery "ahead of SWIFT's November 2025 deadline", remote and offshore teams, and Scrum, Jira, Confluence and MS Project.
3. **Customer channels and payment-gateway integration, plus vendor due diligence.** E-commerce sites with Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay gateways, a news CMS, an API gateway and B2B/B2C platforms are all listed. Vendor selection and SOW negotiation with "decision or joint-decision authority" are also stated. Java and JavaScript are both listed.

A fourth match is AI in production (the AI code-review service). That matches "AI" in the JD's scope list.

**3. Biggest doubt.** Scale and fit. He is a VP at a Tier-1 bank running 25 engineers, and the JD is a lean in-house team at an undisclosed private company paying $8–12k a month. The recruiter will worry about seniority and pay mismatch, and about whether he will work as a hands-on generalist (UI/UX, mobile, chatbots, hiring) rather than as a programme owner. Secondary doubt: "Head of Technology" years. The head-level roles shown are short (about 1.5 years at AI Park, about 1.2 years at VV Technology), and the screening question asks for exactly that number.

**4. Sentences I could not follow, or too technical for HR (quoted exactly).**
1. "Planned and architected a phased coexistence deployment, not a big-bang cutover, for the ISO 20022 (SWIFT MT-to-MX) migration of cross-border payments, taking MX-rich messaging live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November 2025 deadline, and retiring coexistence in August 2026 with no service interruption."
   *Why:* "coexistence", "big-bang cutover", "MT-to-MX" and "MX-rich messaging" are all undefined. It is the lead achievement and an HR reader gets only "something about a SWIFT deadline".
2. "When the two platform lines' schedules conflict, agree MVP scope with the requirement, project and technical leads, confirm dependencies and set a sequence all parties accept."
   *Why:* there is no subject and the tense differs from the bullet above. "The requirement lead" is an unfamiliar role name.
3. "Led end-to-end design of a payment-initiation hub that validates, enriches and tracks payments from digital and branch channels, integrating reference data, third-party banking systems and core banking, which executes settlement."
   *Why:* the final clause "which executes settlement" is ambiguous. It could refer to the hub or to core banking. The list before it is dense.
4. "Designed the middle-platform architecture (Spring Boot, MySQL cluster, Redis, RocketMQ, AWS) for a B2B online-office product serving Southeast Asia, governing solution design, cost evaluation, staffing and acceptance."
   *Why:* "middle-platform" is not a standard English term, and the technology list is not readable by HR.
5. "Established delivery KPIs — deployment frequency, MTTR, incident trend and change-failure rate — with CI/CD quality gates (99% SonarQube coverage gate, automated regression and penetration testing) and SIT/UAT sign-off for every release."
   *Why:* MTTR, CI/CD, SonarQube and SIT/UAT are all unexpanded in one sentence.

Lower-priority jargon, not counted above: "RAID" and "DPIA" in Core Competencies, "customer-360" and "Spring Batch jobs", and "Flume, Hive, MapReduce, Spark Streaming and Spark SQL" as a tool list.

## (b) Hiring manager, about 3 minutes

**1. Can he do this job?**

*Clearly evidenced:*
- Regulated environment (MAS TRM, DPIA, MAS reporting, secure SDLC).
- Leading mixed teams through squad leads and engineering managers, including onshore/offshore.
- BAU plus change in parallel, with prioritisation across two platform lines.
- Vendor due diligence and governance (four IT service vendors, selection and SOW/licence authority).
- Payment gateways (Alipay, WeChat Pay, UnionPay, Visa/Mastercard, Google Pay, GrabPay).
- Content management system (Sohu).
- API gateway (Golden Bridge, AWS API Gateway).
- B2B and B2C platforms.
- Java and JavaScript (React), Jira, Confluence, MS Project, Scrum.
- Deadline record (ISO 20022 ahead of the SWIFT deadline, Baidu order-system re-architecture two months early).
- Production AI (code-review service, AI agent, knowledge assistant).
- Stakeholder management (senior management and steering committees).

*Thin or missing:*
- Hiring, and staff appraisal as a named responsibility. Competencies say "mentoring and performance management", but no hiring example is given.
- UI/UX, mobile application development and chatbots. None is evidenced anywhere in the experience. The AI knowledge assistant is the nearest thing to a chatbot.
- C++ is not mentioned. This is minor, because the JD says "Java and JavaScript" are acceptable examples.
- Running a lean team where he must also code, support and procure. All his recent evidence is a layered organisation (squad leads, engineering managers, 15 + 10 engineers).
- Web-portal development appears only in old roles (Renren, Sohu, Baidu, 2008–2014).
- Data analytics and infrastructure/cloud are present but not recent. Cloud is AWS at Golden Bridge and VV (2019–2022). The UOB bullets mention no cloud.

**2. Hard red flags.**
- **Level and pay.** VP at a Tier-1 bank applying for a role at $8–12k a month.
- **Headline vs title.** The headline says "Head of Technology Development". His current title is VP2 / Technical Delivery Manager. It reads as a target title and not a held title, which is acceptable only if that is clear.
- **Number inconsistencies.**
  - The summary says he leads "25 onshore and offshore engineers". The UOB bullets say "Led a 15-engineer" team and "Lead the 10-engineer backend team". These can be added up, but the reader has to do it.
  - "earlier led VV's 140-person technology organisation (total headcount)" appears only in Achievements. The VV Technology section never states 140. A reader cannot check it.
  - The Golden Bridge section says a "40-person project team", again "(total headcount)", which invites the question of how many reported to him.
- **Dates.**
  - Hengshunyuan (Oct 2017 – Sep 2019) overlaps VV Technology (Apr 2019 – Jun 2020) by about five months. Both are shown as full leadership roles.
  - The timeline jumps from Dec 2014 (Baidu) to Apr 2016 (AI Park). That is a 15-month gap with no explanation.
  - There are also two short gaps: Jun – Nov 2020 and Jul – Oct 2022.
- **Overstatement risk.** "Personally built the hub's core modules", "reviewing new core pull requests daily" and "Head of" claims sit next to "leads 25 engineers". It is plausible but needs probing. "~80%" review time saving is flagged as an estimate, which is good.
- **Tense mix.** Led / Own / Act / Lead in the UOB bullets makes it unclear what is current.
- **Education fit.** The Bachelor's is Applied Mathematics. The JD asks for CS, AI, ML or relevant. The Master's in Computer Science and Technology covers it.

**3. Interview questions.**
1. This is a lean team of a handful of people in a non-bank company. You have run 25 engineers with squad leads and engineering managers. What do you do yourself when there is no layer between you and the engineers, and what is your pay expectation against $8–12k?
2. Tell me about a time you hired and a time you managed someone out or appraised a team member. How many people have you recruited?
3. The JD covers mobile apps, UI/UX and chatbots. What have you personally shipped or owned in each of those areas?
4. Walk me through the 140-person VV organisation: how many reported to you, and how did the VV-Life platform, the big-data team and the B2B office product fit together? How did VV overlap with Hengshunyuan in 2019?
5. What happened between Baidu (Dec 2014) and AI Park (Apr 2016)? And in the AI code-review service, who approved it and how was the "~80%" measured?

**4. Would I interview him?** **Yes.** The regulated-environment, team-leadership, vendor and payment-gateway evidence is strong, and he writes code. The pay and "lean team" fit, and the missing mobile, UI/UX and chatbot evidence, would be tested in the interview.

## Suggestions (wording, order and cuts only)

1. **ISO 20022 bullet (lead achievement).** Replace with plain wording using only the facts already shown:
   > "Led the move of cross-border payments to the new ISO 20022 messaging standard (replacing SWIFT's older MT format). Old and new formats ran side by side in two phases rather than one big switch-over, with no service interruption. Went live in May and August 2025, ahead of SWIFT's November 2025 deadline, and retired the old format in August 2026."
2. **Schedule-conflict bullet.** Replace "When the two platform lines' schedules conflict, agree MVP scope with the requirement, project and technical leads, confirm dependencies and set a sequence all parties accept." with:
   > "Resolve schedule conflicts between the two platform lines by agreeing minimum scope with the requirements, project and technical leads, confirming dependencies and setting a sequence all parties accept."
3. **Payment-hub bullet.** Replace the end of "…integrating reference data, third-party banking systems and core banking, which executes settlement." with:
   > "Led end-to-end design of a payment-initiation hub that validates, enriches and tracks payments from digital and branch channels. The hub connects to reference data, third-party banking systems and core banking, and core banking executes settlement."
4. **KPI bullet.** Replace with:
   > "Set delivery KPIs (deployment frequency, time to restore service, incident trend, change-failure rate) and release quality gates: a 99% code-coverage threshold, automated regression and penetration testing, and system and user-acceptance test sign-off for every release."
5. **VV "middle-platform" bullet.** Replace "middle-platform architecture" with "shared back-end platform architecture". Move the technology list to the end or cut it to "(Spring Boot, MySQL, Redis, RocketMQ, AWS)".
6. **Big-data bullet (cut).** "Led the big-data team on Flume, Hive, MapReduce, Spark Streaming and Spark SQL" becomes "Led the big-data team (Spark, Hive), providing data analysis that supported business decisions."
7. **140-person claim (order).** Move the "140-person technology organisation" figure into the VV Technology section, or cut it from Achievements. As it stands it is stated once where the reader cannot verify it.
8. **Headcounts (wording).** Add the sum in the UOB header line, for example "25 engineers (15 + 10 across the two teams)". Cut "(total headcount)" from the VV and Golden Bridge claims or define it once.
9. **Tense.** Use past tense for finished work and present tense only for current duties, with the UOB bullets in a consistent order (led / own / act → pick one convention).
10. **Order.** Move "Multi-discipline leadership" and "Vendor assessment and governance" above "Content-management system" and "Multi-tenant SaaS" in Selected Achievements. They map to the JD's first and sixth responsibilities and appear after older items now.
11. **Cuts.** Expand or remove "RAID", "DPIA" and "customer-360" in Core Competencies and the UOB section. The "Technical" competency row duplicates the stack already named in the experience bullets.
